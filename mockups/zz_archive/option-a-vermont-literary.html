Featured Poetry
We drove the back roads, windows down,
past the orchard where the last pears
hung low and heavy as secrets.
You said something about the light—
how it pressed against the hills
like a hand on a door about to open.
"The pressure of all that light"
Hawai'i Review

Holly's poetry has appeared in over 90 journals worldwide, from Nimrod and Barrelhouse to the New Zealand Listener and Landfall.


Her work explores queer identity, language, place, and the ways words can be both puzzles and revelations.
