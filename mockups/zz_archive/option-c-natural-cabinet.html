From the Collection

No. 47
We drove the back roads, windows down,
past the orchard where the last pears
hung low and heavy as secrets.
You said something about the light—
how it pressed against the hills
like a hand on a door about to open.
"The pressure of all that light"
Published in Hawai'i Review

Holly's poetry has appeared in over 90 journals worldwide, including Nimrod, Barrelhouse, the New Zealand Listener, and Landfall.


Her work explores queer identity, language as puzzle, the natural world, and the tender archaeology of memory.
