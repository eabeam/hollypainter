Holly Painter is the author of four books of poetry.

[*At last, we listen closely: cryptic crossword poems*](/books/at-last-we-listen-closely-cryptic-crossword-poems/) (Black Spring, 2026) invents an original form: interconnected poems based on cryptic crosswords. [*The pressure of all that light*](/books/the-pressure-of-all-that-light/) (Rebel Satori, 2022) is a poetic exploration of growing up queer and genderqueer. [*My Pet Sounds Off: Translating the Beach Boys*](/books/my-pet-sounds-off/) (Finishing Line, 2020) is a satirical English-to-English translation of Beach Boys hits. [*Excerpts from a Natural History*](/books/excerpts-from-a-natural-history/) (Titus, 2015) is an experimental poetic encyclopedia, counterfactual history of natural philosophy, travelogue, doomed work romance, and meditation on marginalia.

Holly is currently finishing up a collection of oral histories with workers in occupations that are becoming obsolete.

She lives with her wife and two children in Vermont, where she is the Director of the [Center for Research on Vermont](https://www.uvm.edu/cas/vermontresearch) and a Senior Lecturer in the [English Department](https://www.uvm.edu/cas/english/profile/holly-painter) at the University of Vermont.
